# 42 Heilbronn Scholarship — Video Script

*Target length: ~2 minutes. Use this as a guide, not a word-for-word script — it'll sound more natural if you speak it in your own words rather than memorizing it. Look at the camera like you're talking to a person, not reading.*

---

**[Intro — smile, look at the camera]**

Hi, my name is Kyrylo Holovatenko. I'm 19 years old, and I'm from Ukraine. I've been living in Stuttgart, Germany, building a new life here while staying connected to home.

**[Your story]**

Ever since I was a kid, technology has fascinated me. The moment I started programming, it felt like discovering a new way of thinking — one where I could build anything I imagined. Over the years I taught myself Python, made games in Unity, learned web development, and just kept going. Programming isn't just a skill for me — it's where I feel free.

**[Why 42 / transition into motivation]**

I already had the chance to do the Piscine at 42 Heilbronn, and it genuinely changed how I see my future. The people, the environment, the way everyone pushes each other to grow — I've never found anything like it anywhere else. I know this is where I want to build my career.

**[The core ask — financial need]**

Right now, I receive state financial support while I work on getting my footing here. Studying at 42 full-time, more than 40 hours a week, means moving to Heilbronn and being able to focus completely — without splitting my energy between the program and just getting by. This scholarship wouldn't just help me attend. It would let me actually give everything I have to it, the way the program deserves.

**[Closing]**

Everything I know how to do, I taught myself — one project at a time, figuring it out as I went. That's the drive I want to bring into the 42 Heilbronn community. Thank you for considering me.

### Quick filming tips
- Film horizontally, in a well-lit spot facing a window if possible — no need for anything fancy.
- Keep your face centered and close enough to fill most of the frame.
- Do one practice run without recording first, then record 2-3 takes and pick the one that feels most natural.
- If you stumble on a line, just keep going — small imperfections make it feel more genuine, not less.
